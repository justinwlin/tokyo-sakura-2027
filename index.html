The short version

Base yourself near Tokyo Station or the Imperial Palace (Marunouchi or Otemachi) if you want to walk to moat-side cherry trees and catch the Shinkansen to Kyoto easily. Bvlgari, Palace Hotel Tokyo and Four Seasons Otemachi all hold Michelin’s top rating (3 Keys) for 2026. For World of Hyatt points, Park Hyatt (newly renovated) and Andaz are the standouts.

Timing matters more than the hotel. Tokyo usually blooms a few days before Kyoto: normal full bloom is Mar 31 in Tokyo (JMA) and about Apr 4 in Kyoto. With Kyoto around Apr 1–4, doing Tokyo first (about Mar 28–Apr 1) lines up with Tokyo’s normal peak. Tokyo after Kyoto (about Apr 4–7) still works in a late year like 2024 (full bloom Apr 4), but in an early year like 2026 (full bloom Mar 28) the main trees would be shedding by then.

These are Japan Meteorological Agency dates for its sample tree at Yasukuni Shrine, next to Chidorigafuchi. Full bloom (80% open) usually lasts about a week before the petals start to fall. There is no 2027 forecast yet; official forecasts usually start in winter and get accurate in early March.

Best areas to stay

Marunouchi, Otemachi & Nihonbashi

Polished business district around Tokyo Station and the Imperial Palace. Quiet at night, very central.

Sakura & pluses
Easiest Shinkansen to Kyoto. Walk to the palace moats, East Gardens and Chidorigafuchi (about 25–35 min walk or a quick taxi).
Minuses
Few lively streets after 9 pm.
Hotels here
Aman, Four Seasons Otemachi, Palace, Bvlgari, Mandarin Oriental, The Peninsula (Hibiya edge)

Ginza

Tokyo’s luxury shopping street, with department-store food halls, galleries and great restaurants.

Sakura & pluses
Very walkable and central; easy metro to everything.
Minuses
Few cherry trees in the district itself; Chidorigafuchi and Sumida Park are a short taxi ride.
Hotels here
The Tokyo EDITION, Ginza

Toranomon & Azabudai

New towers between Ginza and Roppongi, with Tokyo Tower views and new food halls.

Sakura & pluses
Newest hotels, central location, Shiba Park and Ark Hills Sakurazaka close by.
Minuses
Office-heavy; it still feels a bit corporate.
Hotels here
Andaz, Hotel Toranomon Hills, The Tokyo EDITION Toranomon, Janu

Roppongi & Akasaka

Museums, restaurants and nightlife, plus two big complexes with their own cherry streets.

Sakura & pluses
Night sakura on your doorstep (Midtown, Roppongi Hills), plus Aoyama Cemetery.
Minuses
Roppongi crossing is loud and touristy at night; Akasaka is calmer.
Hotels here
The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Hyatt

Shinjuku

A huge transit hub with skyscrapers in the west and nightlife in the east.

Sakura & pluses
Shinjuku Gyoen is a short taxi ride; Yoyogi Park is close.
Minuses
Massive station crowds; farther from the palace-moat spots and Tokyo Station.
Hotels here
Park Hyatt

Shibuya & Harajuku

Youthful, busy and full of shopping, with Omotesando’s architecture nearby.

Sakura & pluses
Closest to Yoyogi Park and the Meguro River.
Minuses
Fewer top-tier luxury hotels; nothing here made our list.

Cherry blossom spots

Every photo below was taken at that spot during bloom. Night light-up details are from 2026, the most recent season. 2027 dates are announced in early spring.

1 / 5

Chidorigafuchi 千鳥ヶ淵

A tunnel of cherry trees hanging over the Imperial Palace moat, with rowboats underneath. Tokyo’s classic sakura view.

Peak
Usually right on the Tokyo full-bloom date (JMA’s sample tree is at nearby Yasukuni Shrine).
Night / hours
2026: Chidorigafuchi night light-up ran Mar 26–Apr 6, sunset (about 6 pm) to 9 pm. source
Tips
Go early morning for the boats; the rowboat line gets long at midday. The walk continues to Kitanomaru Park and Yasukuni Shrine.
Station
Kudanshita (Hanzomon, Tozai, Shinjuku lines), exit 2

· Open in Google Maps

1 / 5

Shinjuku Gyoen 新宿御苑

A huge landscaped garden with many cherry varieties, so something is usually in bloom from mid-March to late April.

Peak
Early weeping cherries come before the main bloom; double-flowered (yae) varieties come after it.
Night / hours
No night lighting. 2026: open daily Mar 25–Apr 24 (no Monday closures), 9 am–6 pm with last entry at 5:30. Adults pay ¥500, and alcohol is not allowed. Timed tickets were announced for peak weekends (2026: Mar 28–29 and Apr 4–5, for 10 am–4 pm entry). source
Tips
Book a timed ticket if your dates land on a peak weekend. In 2026, entry from 9–10 am needed no reservation even on those days, and early is calmest.
Station
Shinjuku-gyoemmae (Marunouchi line) or Sendagaya (JR)

· Open in Google Maps

1 / 5

Meguro River (Nakameguro) 目黒川

Several kilometers of cherry trees arching over a narrow canal, lined with cafés and food stalls around Nakameguro.

Peak
Tracks the Tokyo full-bloom date.
Night / hours
2026: lights ran from first bloom through Mar 31, 5–8 pm, plus about 15 days of lanterns (chōchin). source
Tips
Weekday mornings are calmest. Evenings at peak are packed, and you can’t stop on the bridges.
Station
Naka-meguro (Hibiya line / Tokyu Toyoko line)

· Open in Google Maps

1 / 5

Ueno Park 上野恩賜公園

Tokyo’s busiest hanami scene: a long avenue of cherry trees with picnic blankets on both sides, plus Shinobazu Pond.

Peak
Tracks the Tokyo full-bloom date.
Night / hours
2026: the Ueno Sakura Festival ran Mar 14–Apr 5, with lanterns and the Shinobazu Pond light-up from 5–10 pm (light-up to Apr 12). source
Tips
Combine it with the museums or Yanaka. It’s lively and loud on weekend evenings, which is part of the fun.
Station
Ueno (JR / Ginza and Hibiya lines), park exit

· Open in Google Maps

1 / 5

Sumida Park (Asakusa) 隅田公園

Cherry trees along both banks of the Sumida River, with Tokyo Skytree behind them. Easy to pair with Senso-ji.

Peak
Tracks the Tokyo full-bloom date.
Night / hours
2026: on the Sumida side, the Bokutei Sakura Festival (Mar 20–Apr 5) lit the trees 6:30–9 pm. On the Taito side, the festival ran Mar 15–Apr 5. source
Tips
Take a river cruise from Asakusa toward Hamarikyu for a different angle.
Station
Asakusa (Ginza / Asakusa lines, Tobu)

1 / 5

Imperial Palace East Gardens & Inui-dori 皇居東御苑・乾通り

Free gardens on the old Edo Castle grounds, with varied cherry trees. In spring the palace also opens Inui-dori, a cherry-lined road inside the walls.

Peak
Several varieties, so a wider window than one big avenue.
Night / hours
No night lighting. Normally closed on Mondays and Fridays (Mar 1–Apr 14: 9 am–5 pm, last entry 4:30). 2026: Inui-dori opened Mar 21–29, with entry 9 am–3:30 pm, and the East Gardens stayed open on those Monday and Friday closure days. source
Tips
Watch for the Inui-dori dates, which are announced each year by the Imperial Household Agency. They are a walk from the Otemachi and Marunouchi hotels.
Station
Otemachi (several lines), a few minutes to Ote-mon gate

Rikugien Garden 六義園

An Edo-period strolling garden famous for one giant weeping cherry near the main gate.

Peak
The weeping cherry blooms earlier than the main Somei-yoshino trees, so it’s often best in the third or fourth week of March.
Night / hours
2026: the spring night viewing ran Mar 18–24, 6:30–9 pm (last entry 8 pm). source
Tips
If you land after the main bloom, this one will likely be past its peak. Check the garden’s bloom reports.
Station
Komagome (JR Yamanote / Namboku line)

A big, relaxed park with picnic lawns under the cherry trees, right next to Harajuku and Meiji Shrine.

No organized light-up.

A good casual picnic spot. Pair it with Meiji Shrine and Omotesando.

Aoyama Cemetery 青山霊園

A quiet avenue of old cherry trees forming a pink tunnel through a historic cemetery. Very local, few tourists.

Peak
Tracks the Tokyo full-bloom date.
Night / hours
No light-up.
Tips
Be respectful: it’s a working cemetery, not a picnic spot. A short walk from the Midtown and Roppongi hotels.

Nogizaka (Chiyoda line) or Aoyama-itchome

Roppongi Hills & Tokyo Midtown 六本木ヒルズ・東京ミッドタウン

Two modern complexes with cherry streets: Midtown’s Sakura-dori and Hinokicho Park, and Roppongi Hills’ Sakurazaka and Mohri Garden.

Peak
Tracks the Tokyo full-bloom date.
Night / hours
2026: during MIDTOWN BLOSSOM (Mar 13–Apr 12), the cherry trees along Midtown’s Sakura-dori were lit 5–11 pm. source

The easiest night sakura if you stay at the Ritz-Carlton or Grand Hyatt: walk out after dinner.

Roppongi (Hibiya / Oedo lines)
